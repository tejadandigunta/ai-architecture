How AI Knows Meaning

Why does AI understand that “car” and “automobile” are almost the same?

01 · What embeddings are

Text becomes numbers

Before text enters a vector database, an embedding model converts it into a list of numbers called a vector.

04 · The important detail

The real map is huge

The simple map above is only an illustration. Real embedding vectors are not just 2D or 3D.

100s–1000s of numbers

Each dimension captures a tiny signal about meaning, context, or relationships.

The takeaway

Embeddings let AI search by meaning, not just keywords.

Words become vectors. Similar ideas become neighbors.
